Grammar · "Use of English" (tasks 19–31)

33 min
Don't confuse the two tasks (although both say "transform the word in CAPITALS"): 3A tasks 19–24 — change only the grammatical form, the part of speech stays the same (work → worked, good → better, child → children). 3B tasks 25–31 — form a NEW word of a different part of speech (fame → famous, decide → decision). Tip: 3A — "which form?", 3B — "which part of speech?".
3A · Grammatical forms (tasks 19–24).

The word in CAPITALS must be put into the correct grammatical form — the part of speech does NOT change. What is tested:

What is testedExample
Verb tense / form (incl. passive)get → got (Past Simple), concentrate → is concentrated (Passive)
Degrees of comparison (adj./adv.)good → best, easy → easier, far → further
Pronouns (subject/possessive/object)they → them, I → my
Plural, incl. irregularschild → children, life → lives
Numerals (ordinal)one → first, two → second
Practice · real FIPI tasks. Put the word into the correct form:
  • 1. You see, setting an example is the way of teaching. (good)
  • 2. About 90% of the world's fresh water in Antarctica. (concentrate)
  • 3. Any planet with an atmosphere can have auroras around . (they)
  • 4. Few people know that Sofia interested in mathematics at a very early age. (get)
  • 5. The speech was so captivating that for the time in their lives the reporters forgot to take notes. (one)
  • 6. love making New Year cards and giving them to their parents. (child)
Answers 3A: 1 best · 2 is concentrated · 3 them · 4 got · 5 first · 6 Children. Note: 2 — passive (the water "is concentrated"), 4 — Past Simple of an irregular verb, 6 — plural + capital letter (start of the sentence). The part of speech never changed — that is what tells 3A apart from 3B. (Tasks 3A/3B are real FIPI tasks, not tied to the topic; they work for any lesson.)
3B · Word formation (tasks 25–31).

You are given a word from the same root — form the required part of speech: ① identify the part of speech from its place in the sentence; ② choose a suffix/prefix; ③ check the meaning (often a negative prefix is needed) and the number.

Part of speechCommon suffixes / prefixesExamples
Noun-tion/-sion, -ment, -ness, -ity, -ence, -ist, -eraccommodate → accommodation, happy → happiness
Adjective-ful, -ous, -ive, -al, -able, -ic, -lessfame → famous, colour → colourful
Adverb-ly (from an adjective)real → really
Negation (prefix)un-, in-, im-, ir-, il-, dis-, non-, inter-inhabited → uninhabited, national → international
Practice · real FIPI tasks. Form the word:
  • 1. The University of Auckland has a strong focus on legal and programmes for students from all over the world. (busy)
  • 2. at the resort ranges from budget to luxury. (accommodate)
  • 3. Whistler is a two-hour drive from Vancouver and is a ski resort. (fame)
  • 4. The streets look magical because they are illuminated with numerous lights. (colour)
  • 5. , free guided tours depart from the entrance hall. (alternative)
  • 6. If you are lucky to see the Northern Lights, you will be impressed. (real)
  • 7. By the age of 19, Tretyak was famous not only in Russia, but also on the level. (nation)
  • 8. You can go on day trips to the remote, islands where no one lives. (inhabited)
Answers 3B: 1 business · 2 Accommodation · 3 famous · 4 colourful · 5 Alternatively · 6 really · 7 international · 8 uninhabited. Traps: 7 — the PREFIX inter- is needed; 8 — the negative prefix un- because of the meaning "where no one lives"; busi-ness and fam-ous drop the "e"/"y". Full practice of grammar and word formation is in the trainer (homework).
4

Writing · Email to a friend (task 37)

40 min

🔗 Bridge. The forms and word formation we have just practised are exactly what "language accuracy" (K3) means. Now we apply it where it earns marks — in a real email to a friend about the Internet.

✉️ Real EGE task. You have received a letter from your pen-friend. Reply to it.

From: Charles@mail.uk  ·  To: Russian_friend@ege.ru  ·  Subject: Internet

…My parents gave me a laptop for my birthday. What do you use the Internet for? What do you usually download from the Internet? Is it popular with Russian teenagers to have their own blogs or websites, why or why not? Yesterday I went with my class on a school trip to the capital…

Write an email to Charles. In your message:
– answer his 3 questions; – ask 3 questions about his school trip. Write 100–140 words. Remember the rules of email writing.

4.1 · Structure (5 parts).
1Greeting + opening. "Dear Charles," + a reaction to the news / thanks for the letter (1–2 sentences). 2Answer his 3 questions. A separate paragraph — answer ALL three questions, 1–2 sentences each, with details and B2 topic vocabulary. 3Ask 3 questions. A separate paragraph — ask 3 questions specifically about the school trip (not general ones!). 4Closing. A closing line + "Best wishes," / "Write back soon," + your name on a new line.
4.2 · Useful phrases.
• Thanks for your email!
• It was great to hear from you.
• Congratulations on your new laptop!
• I mostly use the Internet for…
• I usually download… (films, music, apps)
• Blogs are quite popular because…
• Your school trip sounds exciting! By the way,…
• Where…? How long…? Did you…?
• That's all for now.
• Write back soon and tell me…
Criteria (max 6 marks): K1 content — all 3 answers + 3 on-topic questions, correct format; K2 organisation — salutation, logical paragraphs, linkers, closing; K3 language — accuracy and range of vocabulary/grammar (this is where the topic phrases from Lesson 1 go: at your fingertips, make life easier, digital footprint!).
4.3 · Editing (K3): find and correct the error. Typical B2 errors in an email about technology — write the correct version:
  • 1. Scientists are looking forward to discover new planets. →
  • 2. They have invented this device two years ago. →
  • 3. The article gave us new informations about robots. →
  • 4. This program help students learn faster. →
  • 5. She said me about the new app. →
  • 6. The robot was enough smart to solve it. →
Answers (K3, B2): 1 discovering; 2 invented; 3 information; 4 helps; 5 told me; 6 smart enough. Error type: 1 gerund after to; 2 Past Simple with ago; 3 uncountable noun; 4 third person singular; 5 tell sb (not say sb); 6 adjective + enough.
4.4 · Write your email.
0 words
Model answer (≈125 words):
Dear Charles,
Thanks for your email! Congratulations on your new laptop — I'm sure it will make your studies much easier.
I mostly use the Internet for schoolwork, staying in touch with friends and watching films in the evening. I usually download music, apps and the occasional series, though I try to be careful about my digital footprint. Blogs and personal channels are really popular with Russian teenagers, because everyone wants to share content and a video can easily go viral overnight.
Your school trip to the capital sounds exciting! Where exactly did you go? How long did the trip last? Did you visit any museums there?
That's all for now. Write back soon and tell me all about it!
Best wishes,
Alex
Walk-through with the student: mark the 3 answers + 3 questions (K1), the paragraphs and linkers (K2), the topic phrases digital footprint, go viral, stay in touch, make life easier (K3). Let students write their own email and submit it for AI marking in the trainer.
