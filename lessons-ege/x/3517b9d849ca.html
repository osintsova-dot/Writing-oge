Grammar · "Use of English" (tasks 19–31)

33 min
Don't confuse the two tasks (both say "change the word in CAPITALS"): 3A tasks 19–24 — change only the grammatical form, the part of speech stays the same (work → worked, good → better, child → children). 3B tasks 25–31 — form a NEW word of a different part of speech (fame → famous, decide → decision). Tip: 3A — "which form?", 3B — "which part of speech?".
3A · Grammatical forms (tasks 19–24).

The word in CAPITALS must be put into the correct grammatical form — the part of speech does NOT change. What is tested:

What is testedExample
Verb tense / form (incl. passive)get → got (Past Simple), concentrate → is concentrated (Passive)
Degrees of comparison (adj./adv.)good → best, easy → easier, far → further
Pronouns (subject/possessive/object)they → them, I → my
Plural, incl. irregularschild → children, life → lives
Numerals (ordinal)one → first, two → second
Practice · real FIPI tasks. Put the word into the correct form:
  • 1. You see, setting an example is the way of teaching. (good)
  • 2. About 90% of the world's fresh water in Antarctica. (concentrate)
  • 3. Any planet with an atmosphere can have auroras around . (they)
  • 4. Few people know that Sofia interested in mathematics at a very early age. (get)
  • 5. The speech was so captivating that for the time in their lives the reporters forgot to take notes. (one)
  • 6. love making New Year cards and giving them to their parents. (child)
Answers 3A: 1 best · 2 is concentrated · 3 them · 4 got · 5 first · 6 Children. Note: 2 — passive, 4 — Past Simple of an irregular verb, 6 — plural + capital letter (start of the sentence). The part of speech never changed — this is exactly what distinguishes 3A from 3B. (Real FIPI tasks; forms/word formation are not tied to the topic.)
3B · Word formation (tasks 25–31).

You are given a word from the same root — form the required part of speech: ① identify the part of speech from its place in the sentence; ② choose a suffix/prefix; ③ check the meaning (often you need a negative prefix) and number.

Part of speechCommon suffixes / prefixesExamples
Noun-tion/-sion, -ment, -ness, -ity, -ence, -ist, -eraccommodate → accommodation, happy → happiness
Adjective-ful, -ous, -ive, -al, -able, -ic, -lessfame → famous, colour → colourful
Adverb-ly (from an adjective)real → really
Negation (prefix)un-, in-, im-, ir-, il-, dis-, non-, inter-inhabited → uninhabited, national → international

  • 3. Whistler is a two-hour drive from Vancouver and is a ski resort. (fame)
  • 4. The streets look magical because they are illuminated with numerous lights. (colour)
  • 5. , free guided tours depart from the entrance hall. (alternative)
  • 6. If you are lucky to see the Northern Lights, you will be impressed. (real)
  • 7. By the age of 19, Tretyak was famous not only in Russia, but also on the level. (nation)

Writing · Email to a friend (task 37)

40 min

🔗 Bridge. The forms and word formation we have just practised are exactly what "language accuracy" (K3) means. Now we apply it where it earns marks — in a real email about the "work or study" choice.

✉️ Real EGE task. You have received a letter from a pen-friend. Write a reply.

From: Sam@mail.uk  ·  To: Russian_friend@ege.ru  ·  Subject: Work or study

…I really don't know whether to enter a college or to start working after finishing school. What are your plans? Do many Russian school-leavers continue their education in universities, why? Where can a teenager work in Russia after finishing school? My elder sister is getting married next week, it will be a big wedding…

Write an email to Sam. In your message:
– answer his 3 questions; – ask 3 questions about his sister's wedding. Write 100–140 words. Remember the rules of email writing.

4.1 · Structure (5 parts).
1Greeting + opening. "Dear Sam," + a reaction to the news / thanks for the letter (1–2 sentences). 2Answer his 3 questions. A separate paragraph — answer ALL three questions (your plans; why school-leavers go to university; where a teenager can work), 1–2 sentences each, with details and B2 vocabulary. 3Ask 3 questions. A separate paragraph — ask 3 questions specifically about his sister's wedding (not general ones!). 4Closing. A closing phrase + "Best wishes," / "Write back soon," + your name on a new line.
4.2 · Useful phrases.
• Thanks for your email!
• It was great to hear from you.
• As for my plans, I'm going to…
• Many school-leavers continue their education because…
• Teenagers in Russia can work as…
• I'd rather… because…
• That's exciting news about the wedding! By the way,…
• Where…? How many…? What will…?
• That's all for now.
• Write back soon and tell me…
Criteria (max 6 marks): K1 content — all 3 answers + 3 on-topic questions, correct format; K2 organisation — salutation, logical paragraphs, linkers, ending; K3 language — accuracy and range of vocabulary/grammar (this is where the career phrases from Lesson 1 go!).
4.3 · Editing (K3): find and correct the error. Typical B2 mistakes in a work/study email — write the correct version:
  • 1. I enjoy to work with people. →
  • 2. She wants to be architect. →
  • 3. A good manager is responsible of the team. →
  • 4. My father work as a doctor. →
  • 5. After a hard day I feel tiring. →
  • 6. I've worked here since five years. →
Answers (K3, B2): 1 working; 2 an architect; 3 for; 4 works; 5 tired; 6 for. Error type: 1 enjoy + -ing; 2 article before a profession; 3 responsible FOR; 4 third person singular; 5 -ed for a person; 6 for + period of time.
4.4 · Write your email.
0 words
Model (≈125 words):
Dear Sam,
Thanks for your email! It's a tricky choice, but I'm sure you'll weigh up the pros and cons and make the right decision.
As for my plans, I've set my heart on entering university to study engineering, because it feels like a real stepping stone to a rewarding career. Many Russian school-leavers continue their education at universities, mainly because a degree helps you land a job with a competitive salary. After school, teenagers here can work as couriers, shop assistants or waiters, or do summer jobs to gain hands-on experience.
Your sister's wedding sounds wonderful! Where will it take place? How many guests are coming? What are you going to wear?
That's all for now. Write back soon!
Best wishes,
Alex
Walk-through with the student: highlight the 3 answers + 3 questions (K1), the paragraphs and linkers (K2), the career phrases weigh up the pros and cons, set my heart on, stepping stone, land a job, gain hands-on experience (K3). Have students write their own email and submit it for AI marking in the trainer.
