Grammar · "Use of English" (tasks 19–31)

33 min
Don't confuse the two tasks (even though both say "change the word in CAPITALS"): 3A tasks 19–24 — you only change the grammatical form, the part of speech stays the same (work → worked, good → better, child → children). 3B tasks 25–31 — you form a NEW word of a different part of speech (fame → famous, decide → decision). Tip: 3A asks "which form?", 3B asks "which part of speech?".
3A · Grammatical forms (tasks 19–24).

The word in CAPITALS must be put into the correct grammatical form — the part of speech does NOT change. What is tested:

What is testedExample
Verb tense / form (incl. passive)get → got (Past Simple), concentrate → is concentrated (Passive)
Degrees of comparison (adj./adv.)good → best, easy → easier, far → further
Pronouns (subject/possessive/object)they → them, I → my
Plural, incl. irregularschild → children, life → lives
Numerals (ordinal)one → first, two → second
Practice · real FIPI tasks. Put the word into the correct form:
  • 1. You see, setting an example is the way of teaching. (good)
  • 2. About 90% of the world's fresh water in Antarctica. (concentrate)
  • 3. Any planet with an atmosphere can have auroras around . (they)
  • 4. Few people know that Sofia interested in mathematics at a very early age. (get)
  • 5. The speech was so captivating that for the time in their lives the reporters forgot to take notes. (one)
  • 6. love making New Year cards and giving them to their parents. (child)
Answers 3A: 1 best · 2 is concentrated · 3 them · 4 got · 5 first · 6 Children. Note: 2 is the passive, 4 is the Past Simple of an irregular verb, 6 is plural + capital letter (start of the sentence). The part of speech never changes — that is what distinguishes 3A from 3B. (Tasks 3A/3B are topic-independent — these are real FIPI positions, tasks 19–31; the topical focus of the lesson is on the writing.)
3B · Word formation (tasks 25–31).

You are given a word with the same root — form the required part of speech: ① identify the part of speech from its place in the sentence; ② choose the suffix/prefix; ③ check the meaning (often a negative prefix is needed) and number.

Part of speechCommon suffixes / prefixesExamples
Noun-tion/-sion, -ment, -ness, -ity, -ence, -ist, -eraccommodate → accommodation, happy → happiness
Adjective-ful, -ous, -ive, -al, -able, -ic, -lessfame → famous, colour → colourful
Adverb-ly (from an adjective)real → really
Negation (prefix)un-, in-, im-, ir-, il-, dis-, non-, inter-inhabited → uninhabited, national → international
Practice · real FIPI tasks. Form the word:
  • 1. The University of Auckland has a strong focus on legal and programmes for students from all over the world. (busy)
  • 2. at the resort ranges from budget to luxury. (accommodate)
  • 3. Whistler is a two-hour drive from Vancouver and is a ski resort. (fame)
  • 4. The streets look magical because they are illuminated with numerous lights. (colour)
  • 5. , free guided tours depart from the entrance hall. (alternative)
  • 6. If you are lucky to see the Northern Lights, you will be impressed. (real)
  • 7. By the age of 19, Tretyak was famous not only in Russia, but also on the level. (nation)
  • 8. You can go on day trips to the remote, islands where no one lives. (inhabited)
Answers 3B: 1 business · 2 Accommodation · 3 famous · 4 colourful · 5 Alternatively · 6 really · 7 international · 8 uninhabited. Traps: 7 needs the PREFIX inter-; 8 needs the negative prefix un- because of the meaning "where no one lives"; busi-ness and fam-ous drop the "e"/"y". 4

Writing · Email to a friend (task 37)

40 min

🔗 Bridge. The forms and word formation we have just practised are exactly what "language accuracy" (K3) means. Now we apply it where it earns marks — in a real email to a friend about hobbies.

✉️ Real EGE task. You have received a letter from your pen friend. Write a reply.

From: Lily@mail.uk  ·  To: Russian_friend@ege.ru  ·  Subject: Hobbies

…Many of my friends enjoy roller-skating in their free time, and I like it too. How do you and your friends spend free time in your region? Which hobbies are popular with teens in Russia? What new hobby would you like to try in the future, and why this one? Last week I returned from a sports youth camp…

Write an email to Lily. In your message:
– answer her 3 questions; – ask 3 questions about her sports youth camp. Write 100–140 words. Remember the rules of email writing.

4.1 · Structure (4 parts).
1Greeting + opening. "Dear Lily," + a reaction to the news / thanks for the letter (1–2 sentences). 2Answer her 3 questions. A separate paragraph — answer ALL three questions, 1–2 sentences each, with details and the B2 vocabulary from Lesson 1. 3Ask 3 questions. A separate paragraph — ask 3 questions specifically about the sports youth camp (not general ones!). 4Closing. A closing line + "Best wishes," / "Write back soon," + your name on a new line.
4.2 · Useful phrases.
• Thanks for your email!
• It was great to hear from you.
• Roller-skating sounds like great fun!
• In my free time I usually… to unwind.
• The most popular hobbies with teens are…
• In the future I'd love to take up… because…
• Your camp sounds amazing! By the way,…
• Where exactly…? Which sports…? Would you…?
• That's all for now.
• Write back soon and tell me…
Criteria (max 6 marks): K1 content — all 3 answers + 3 on-topic questions, correct format; K2 organisation — greeting, logical paragraphs, linkers, closing; K3 language — accuracy and range of vocabulary/grammar (this is where the Lesson 1 phrases go: unwind, a creative outlet, strike a healthy balance).
4.3 · Editing (K3): find and correct the error. Typical B2 errors in an email about hobbies — write the correct version:
  • 1. Could you tell me where is the chess club? →
  • 2. In my free time I enjoy to paint. →
  • 3. I'm really interested on photography. →
  • 4. I don't have much free hours at the weekend. →
  • 5. I've played the guitar since five years. →
  • 6. This puzzle is more easier than the last one. →
Answers (K3, B2): 1 where the chess club is; 2 painting; 3 in; 4 many; 5 for; 6 easier. Error type: 1 indirect question; 2 enjoy + -ing; 3 interested IN; 4 many with countable nouns; 5 for + period; 6 double comparative.
4.4 · Write your email.
0 words
Model answer (≈120 words):
Dear Lily,
Thanks for your email! Roller-skating sounds like great fun.
In my region, my friends and I usually spend our free time cycling in the park, playing sports and meeting in cafes; when it's cold, we just unwind at home and recharge our batteries. The most popular hobbies with Russian teens are gaming, drawing and dancing. In the future I'd love to take up rock climbing, because it looks exciting and would really push me to the limit.
Your sports youth camp sounds amazing! Where exactly was the camp? Which sports did you do there? Would you go again next summer?
That's all for now. Write back soon and tell me all about it!
Best wishes,
Alex
Analysis with the student: mark the 3 answers + 3 questions (K1), the paragraphs and linkers (K2), the phrases unwind, recharge our batteries, take up, push to the limit (K3). Let students write their own email and submit it for AI marking in the trainer.
