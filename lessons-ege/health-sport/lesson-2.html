Grammar · "Use of English" (tasks 19–31)

33 min
Don’t confuse the two tasks (both say "transform the word in CAPITALS"): 3A tasks 19–24 — change only the grammatical form, the part of speech stays the same (work → worked, good → better, child → children). 3B tasks 25–31 — form a NEW word of a different part of speech (fame → famous, decide → decision). Tip: 3A — "which form?", 3B — "which part of speech?".
3A · Grammatical forms (tasks 19–24).

The word in CAPITALS must be put into the correct grammatical form — the part of speech does NOT change. What is tested:

What is testedExample
Verb tense / form (incl. passive)get → got (Past Simple), concentrate → is concentrated (Passive)
Degrees of comparison (adj./adv.)good → best, easy → easier, far → further
Pronouns (subject/possessive/object)they → them, I → my
Plural, incl. irregularschild → children, life → lives
Numerals (ordinal)one → first, two → second
Practice · real FIPI tasks. Put the word into the correct form:
  • 1. You see, setting an example is the way of teaching. (good)
  • 2. About 90% of the world's fresh water in Antarctica. (concentrate)
  • 3. Any planet with an atmosphere can have auroras around . (they)
  • 4. Few people know that Sofia interested in mathematics at a very early age. (get)
  • 5. The speech was so captivating that for the time in their lives the reporters forgot to take notes. (one)
  • 6. love making New Year cards and giving them to their parents. (child)
Answers 3A: 1 best · 2 is concentrated · 3 them · 4 got · 5 first · 6 Children. Note: 2 — passive, 4 — Past Simple of an irregular verb, 6 — plural + capital letter (start of the sentence). The part of speech never changes — that is what sets 3A apart from 3B. (Real FIPI tasks; not tied to the topic.)
3B · Word formation (tasks 25–31).

You are given a word from the same root — form the required part of speech: ① identify the part of speech from its position in the sentence; ② choose the suffix/prefix; ③ check the meaning (often a negative prefix is needed) and the number.

Part of speechCommon suffixes / prefixesExamples
Noun-tion/-sion, -ment, -ness, -ity, -ence, -ist, -eraccommodate → accommodation, happy → happiness
Adjective-ful, -ous, -ive, -al, -able, -ic, -lessfame → famous, colour → colourful
Adverb-ly (from an adjective)real → really
Negation (prefix)un-, in-, im-, ir-, il-, dis-, non-, inter-inhabited → uninhabited, national → international
Practice · real FIPI tasks. Form the word:
  • 1. The University of Auckland has a strong focus on legal and programmes for students from all over the world. (busy)
  • 2. at the resort ranges from budget to luxury. (accommodate)
  • 3. Whistler is a two-hour drive from Vancouver and is a ski resort. (fame)
  • 4. The streets look magical because they are illuminated with numerous lights. (colour)
  • 5. , free guided tours depart from the entrance hall. (alternative)
  • 6. If you are lucky to see the Northern Lights, you will be impressed. (real)
  • 7. By the age of 19, Tretyak was famous not only in Russia, but also on the level. (nation)
  • 8. You can go on day trips to the remote, islands where no one lives. (inhabited)
Answers 3B: 1 business · 2 Accommodation · 3 famous · 4 colourful · 5 Alternatively · 6 really · 7 international · 8 uninhabited. Traps: 7 — needs the PREFIX inter-; 8 — the negative prefix un- because of the meaning "where no one lives"; busi-ness and fam-ous drop the "e"/"y". 4

Writing · Email to a friend (task 37)

40 min

🔗 Bridge. The forms and word formation we have just practised are exactly what "language accuracy" (K3) means. Now we apply it where it earns marks — in a real email to a friend about health.

✉️ Real EGE task. You have received a letter from your pen friend. Reply to it.

From: Alice@mail.uk  ·  To: Russian_friend@ege.ru  ·  Subject: Healthy lifestyle

…I've decided to go on a diet — I want to lose a few pounds. What are the most efficient ways to lose weight, in your opinion? Why do many people try to follow a healthy lifestyle? How important is your family's support if you choose a healthy lifestyle as your goal? Yesterday my friends and I went to the cinema to watch a new film…

Write an email to Alice. In your message:
– answer her 3 questions; – ask 3 questions about the new film. Write 100–140 words. Remember the rules of email writing.

4.1 · Structure (5 parts).
1Greeting + opening. "Dear Alice," + a reaction to the news / thanks for the email (1–2 sentences). 2Answer her 3 questions. A separate paragraph — answer ALL three questions, 1–2 sentences each, with details and B2 health vocabulary. 3Ask 3 questions. A separate paragraph — ask 3 questions specifically about the new film (not general ones!). 4Closing. A closing line + "Best wishes," / "Write back soon," + your name on a new line.
4.2 · Useful phrases.
• Thanks for your email!
• It's great that you've decided to…
• In my opinion, the best way to lose weight is…
• People follow a healthy lifestyle because…
• Family support matters a lot because…
• A balanced diet and regular exercise…
• Your trip to the cinema sounds fun! By the way,…
• What kind of…? Who…? Would you…?
• That's all for now.
• Write back soon and tell me…
Criteria (max 6 marks): K1 content — all 3 answers + 3 on-topic questions, correct format; K2 organisation — greeting, logical paragraphs, linkers, closing; K3 language — accuracy and range of vocabulary/grammar (this is where the Lesson 1 phrases come in: a balanced diet, keep fit, couch potato!).
4.3 · Editing (K3): find and correct the mistake. Typical mistakes in an email — write the correct version:
  • 1. If I will train every day, I'll get fitter. →
  • 2. My coach gave me good advices. →
  • 3. Swimming is more healthier than sitting at home. →
  • 4. My sister go to the gym every morning. →
  • 5. Your fitness depends of your daily habits. →
  • 6. After the long race I felt really exciting. →
Answers (K3, B2): 1 train; 2 advice; 3 healthier; 4 goes; 5 depends on; 6 excited. Type of mistake: 1 first conditional — no will; 2 uncountable advice; 3 double comparative; 4 third person singular; 5 depend ON; 6 -ed for a person.
4.4 · Write your email.
0 words
Model (≈125 words):
Dear Alice,
Thanks for your email! It's great that you've decided to take better care of your health.
In my opinion, the most efficient way to lose weight is to combine a balanced diet with regular exercise, rather than starving yourself. People follow a healthy lifestyle because it boosts the immune system, improves their mood and gives them more energy. Family support matters a lot — when everyone keeps fit together, it's far easier not to become a couch potato.
Your trip to the cinema sounds fun! What film did you watch? Who starred in it? Would you recommend it to me?
That's all for now. Write back soon!
Best wishes,
Alex
Walk-through with the student: point out the 3 answers + 3 questions (K1), the paragraphs and linkers (K2), the phrases a balanced diet, boost the immune system, keep fit, couch potato (K3 — straight from Lesson 1). Let students write their own email and submit it for AI marking in the trainer.
