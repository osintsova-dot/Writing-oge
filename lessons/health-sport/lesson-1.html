Урок 1 — Лексика и чтение · A2–B1Health & sport: keep fit, stay healthy

Цели: освоить ключевую лексику темы «здоровье и спорт» (14 фраз с переводом и примерами); потренировать чтение ОГЭ — «Верно / Неверно / Не сказано» (зад.13–19) и стратегию работы с утверждениями; потренировать аудирование на реальном задании ФИПИ. Дома: «Лексика» и «Чтение» в ОГЭ-тренажёре по теме.
1

Lead-in · Разговор

8 мин
  • What do you do to keep fit? How often do you exercise?
  • Do you think your diet is healthy? Why / why not?
  • What is your favourite sport — to do or to watch? Why?
Учителю: цель — разговорить и поднять знакомую лексику. На A2–B1 поощряйте полные ответы (I keep fit by… because…). Запишите слова, которые всплывут, — пригодятся в задании 2.

Лексика · ключевые фразы темы

28 мин
2.1 · Изучи фразы (англ. → определение и перевод).

2.2 · Матчинг. Восстанови фразу по определению:
  • 1. to stay strong and healthy by exercising → to
  • 2. to keep away from unhealthy quick meals → to
  • 3. to deal with worry and pressure → to
  • 4. to sleep as much as your body needs → to
  • 5. to start doing a new sport → to a sport
2.3 · Подстановка в контекст.
  • 1. I try to — at least three times a week.
  • 2. A gives your body all the vitamins it needs.
  • 3. In winter I often and have to stay at home.
  • 4. Doing sport is a great way to before exams.
2.4 · Сделай своим. Составь 4 предложения о своём образе жизни с этими фразами: что делаешь, чтобы keep fit, что ешь, как спорт помогает fight stress. Эти же слова отрабатываются в тренажёре (интервальное повторение) и пригодятся в письме и устной части.
Ответы 2.2: keep fit · avoid fast food · fight stress · get enough sleep · take up. 2.3: exercise regularly · balanced diet · catch a cold · fight stress. Лексику добивают в ОГЭ-тренажёре (SRS) — на уроке только ядро (14 фраз).

Чтение · ОГЭ (зад.13–19: Верно / Неверно / Не сказано)

24 мин

🔎 Реальный текст ФИПИ. Прочитай текст и отметь для каждого утверждения: 1 — Верно (True), 2 — Неверно (False), 3 — Не сказано (Not stated).

Running the Thames. Three friends Alan, Tom and Martin decided to run 184 miles along the most famous British river – from the beginning of the Thames down to its mouth, where the river runs into the sea. Their preparations for the marathon didn't take long and they set out. It was not easy to find the place marked on the map as the official source of the river Thames. In reality, it was an old stone under a big tree. Surprisingly, there was practically no water at the source, just a small pit. However, at the end of the first day they came to a narrow stream running under a wooden bridge. The runners were lucky with the weather; it was cool and bright for the whole week. Soft winds blew and the sun shone. In spite of the weather, the start of the marathon was not very promising. On the first day, Martin accidentally fell over a tree root and hurt his foot. In addition to that, Alan said he had damaged his rucksack during their preparation. However, the friends went on running. They passed lovely houses and some wonderful nature. The Thames had grown from a small river into a strong stream. Hypnotised by the view of the river, Tom missed the path and fell into the mud. Cold and angry, he was a real trouble the first day. In the evening, the runners reached the point where the river became possible to navigate. The friends were so tired that they stopped at a cheap hotel for food, drink and sleep. The second day they ran along arched stone bridges and through forty-five river locks, each one having its own history. The runners stopped at the first lock to take a photo by the 19th century statue of Old Father Thames. The other locks and the bridges, dating from the 13th century, could have told much about British royal families, writers and inventors, battles and victories. So the runners spent the second day exploring the locks and the old bridges and taking photos. The next few days Alan, Tom and Martin ran through quiet and peaceful places in Oxfordshire. The calm river occupied all their thoughts and they left their troubles behind them. They spent nights at small Bed-and-Breakfasts with unusual foreign guests who were discovering the old British towns, castles and churches. Closer to London the Thames changed its character. It became wide and busy with numerous boats and ships. The banks were full of grand houses with green lawns, street markets and modern walkways. On the seventh day the runners followed the river as it cut the capital in two. The friends made their way past famous London sights such as The Tower, The London Eye and Greenwich. Finally the friends got to The Thames Barrier, where the river ran into the sea. The marathon was over.

13. There was a natural mark which indicated the start of the river Thames.
14. During the marathon the weather was fine.
15. After the accident with Martin's leg, Tom carried his rucksack.
16. During the marathon the friends spent all their nights in the tent.
17. There were a lot of tourists near the statue of Old Father Thames.
18. Near London the Thames was closed to navigation.
19. The marathon route went through London.
Стратегия. «Не сказано» — самый коварный вариант. Правило: Неверно = текст ПРЯМО противоречит утверждению; Не сказано = в тексте об этом НЕТ информации (ни «да», ни «нет»). Не достраивай по логике — отвечай только по тексту.
Реальный текст ФИПИ («Running the Thames» — трое друзей бегут марафон вдоль Темзы; в нём же ядро лексики темы: run a marathon, set out, be lucky with the weather, hurt one's foot, go on running, reach the finishing point). Ответы: 13→1 · 14→1 · 15→3 · 16→2 · 17→3 · 18→2 · 19→1. На каждом разберите: где подтверждение (True/False) или почему «Не сказано».

Аудирование · ОГЭ (короткие диалоги)

17 мин

🎧 Реальное аудио ФИПИ. Услышишь 4 коротких разговора (спорт, школа, досуг). Прослушай дважды и выбери 1, 2 или 3.

1. What activity is available for the language school students at the moment?
2. Thomas didn't come to support his football team because …
3. Ann's order in the café is a cup of green tea and …
4. What birthday present did Mia get?
Стратегия. Перед прослушиванием прочитай вопрос и варианты, подчеркни ключевые слова. Верный ответ ПЕРЕФРАЗИРУЕТ запись (swimming competition → some other competition), а ловушка часто повторяет слово дословно, но смысл другой.
Реальное аудио ФИПИ (4 коротких диалога). Ответы: 1→1 · 2→3 · 3→3 · 4→2. Разберите, какими словами подтверждается каждый ответ. Аудирование обязательно отрабатывается в тренажёре (ДЗ).
